<div class="eyebrow">THE STANDARD / OUR PURPOSE</div>

Palisade is an independent mathematics education project for exceptionally ambitious students. Its aim is to cultivate world-class mathematical thinkers through an uncompromising commitment to intellectual excellence.

## Mastery over memorization

An answer is a beginning. Understanding means explaining why it is true, recognizing when it applies, and reconstructing the reasoning without a script.

## Depth over breadth

The goal is a coherent education, not a large catalog. Original curricula will prioritize conceptual depth, proof, abstraction, and independent problem-solving. Clear explanations should do their work without unnecessary verbosity.

## Excellence by default

Practice will range from foundational to exceptionally challenging. Mastery will be demonstrated through demanding unit examinations and two equivalent final examination variants.

## A quiet place to think

All instruction will be text-based, free, and self-paced. No video lectures. Mathematical notation, deliberate reading, and uninterrupted reasoning sit at the center of the experience.

## The academic path

| Level | Designation |
| --- | --- |
| 1.X | Preparatory |
| 2.X | Foundational |
| 3.X | Intermediate |
| 4.X | Advanced |
| 5.X | Specialized |

The initial scope is six mathematics courses, beginning with **1.1 — Precalculus**. Academic quality and curricular coherence take priority over catalog expansion.

Inspired by the open access of MIT OpenCourseWare, Palisade intends to build original curricula with exceptional depth and rigor. It is an independent project, with no institutional affiliation. These are design standards and ambitions; no course content is available yet.
